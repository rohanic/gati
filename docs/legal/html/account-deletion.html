Delete your account and data

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your Gati account — the Android app published by Rohan Ahmad (package com.gati.numberswanders) — and exactly what happens to your data when you do.

You may not need an account at all. Gati works fully without one, storing everything on your phone. If you never signed in, we hold no data about you — simply uninstalling the app removes everything.

Option 1 — delete it yourself, in the app

The fastest route. Takes a few seconds and is immediate.

  1. Open Gati and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to Cloud backup and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm twice. The second prompt is the final one — deletion begins as soon as you accept it.
  4. Uninstall the app to also clear anything still stored on your phone.

Option 2 — ask us to delete it

If you have lost access to the app or your device, email us from the address on your Gati account so we can verify the request. Include the word “delete” in the subject.

Email contact@creaeza.com

We respond within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days of verifying the request.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted permanently

  • Your account and email address
  • Your profile — name, date of birth, and all lifestyle answers
  • Which numbers you opened, and when
  • Your streak history
  • Saved places, visits and your place ratings
  • Notes from your Story timeline
  • Your push notification token and time zone
  • Your subscription records

Kept, and why

  • Anonymous place scores. Your individual ratings are erased. The aggregate quality score they contributed to remains, because it contains no identifier and cannot be traced to any person.
  • Payment records held by Google. We do not store your payment details and cannot delete Google’s records. Google retains these for tax and accounting purposes under their own policy.

Deletion is immediate and applies to live systems at once. Encrypted backups are rotated out within 30 days, after which no copy remains.

Before you delete

Cancel your subscription first. Deleting your Gati account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription, and you will continue to be billed. Cancel it in the Play Store → Subscriptions, then delete your account.

This cannot be undone. There is no recovery window and no way for us to restore the data afterwards. If you want a copy, use Profile → Export your data before deleting.

Deleting the app only

Uninstalling Gati removes everything stored on your phone, but it does not delete an account you created. To remove data from our servers, use one of the two options above.
