Data Safety

A plain-language summary of what Gati collects, matching the Data Safety declaration submitted to Google Play. If you want the legal detail, read the Privacy Policy.

Data sold or shared None
Encrypted in transit Yes, all of it

An account is optional. Gati works fully signed out, keeping everything on your phone. Nothing in the table below is collected until you choose to sign in — except approximate location, which is used only while you are looking at nearby places and is never stored.

About your location

This is the part most apps are vague about, so to be specific:

  • Location is read only while the app is open and you are using Wander.
  • It is never read in the background. The app does not hold the Android background-location permission at all.
  • Your position is not written to any database and is not attached to your account.
  • Search results are cached against a coordinate rounded to roughly one kilometre, which cannot identify a person or a home.
  • No location trail or history is ever built.

What we never collect

  • Precise location, background location or location history
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, videos, audio or files
  • Health or fitness sensor data — Gati’s numbers are arithmetic on your own answers, not measurements
  • Messages or browsing history
  • Advertising or device identifiers
  • Behavioural analytics — no analytics SDK is installed

Who else sees your data

Nobody buys it, and nobody receives it for their own purposes. We use a small number of service providers who process data only on our instructions: Supabase (database and sign-in), Google Play (sign-in and billing), Google Places (place search), Firebase (notification delivery) and OpenAI (short place descriptions).

Google Places and OpenAI receive nothing about you — only an approximate coordinate, or a place name and category.

Deleting everything

In the app: Profile → Delete account. Full instructions, including what is deleted and what is kept, are on the account deletion page.
