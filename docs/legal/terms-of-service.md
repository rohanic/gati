## 1. What Gati is

Gati calculates statistics about your life from information you provide during
setup, reveals one each day, and suggests real places near you.

**The numbers are estimates, not measurements.** They are arithmetic applied to
the answers you gave, using published population averages for things you were
not asked about — a heart rate of roughly 70 bpm, around 16 breaths a minute,
about 8,000 steps a day adjusted for how active you said you are.

Gati does not measure your body. It is **not a medical, fitness or diagnostic
tool**, and nothing in it is health advice. Do not make health decisions based
on it. If a number concerns you, speak to a doctor.
