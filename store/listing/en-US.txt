──────────────────────────────────────────────
APP NAME  (Play limit: 30 characters)
──────────────────────────────────────────────
Gati: Life Numbers & Wanders

  Alternates:
    Gati: Life Numbers & Places    (27)  ← more search volume
    Gati: Numbers & Wanders        (23)
    Gati Numbers & Wander          (21)

  Why this one: "Gati" alone is unsearchable — nobody types it. This
  suffix carries both halves of the product, matches the package name
  (com.gati.numberswanders), and "Life Numbers" is the part people
  actually type. No emoji, no ALL CAPS, no "#1"/"Best" — all of those
  are title violations.

  Worth knowing: "Wanders" is the in-app feature name rather than a
  search term; "Places" would pull more traffic. "Life Numbers" earns
  most of that back, which is why this title is the better trade. If
  installs are slower than expected, swapping "Wanders" for "Places"
  is the first thing to try — the title can be changed at any time,
  the package name cannot.

SHORT DESCRIPTION  (Play limit: 80 characters)
──────────────────────────────────────────────
One surprising number about your life each day, and a good place near you.

  Alternates:
    One number about your life each day — then somewhere real to go with it.
    Your life in 25 numbers. One a day. One good place nearby to take it.

  Carries both halves of the product AND the two things people actually
  type: "life" numbers, and places "near you". The previous version said
  "somewhere real to go", which reads well and matches nothing anyone
  searches for.

  This is the line under the icon in search results and the single
  biggest lever on install rate. It should say what happens, not
  what the app "is".

FULL DESCRIPTION  (Play limit: 4000 characters)
──────────────────────────────────────────────
You have taken about 300 million breaths. Your heart has beaten
over 2 billion times. You have slept through roughly a third of
your life, and you have spent whole months of it looking at a
screen.

You know none of these numbers. Gati works them out.

ONE NUMBER A DAY

Gati holds 25 numbers about your life. You do not get them all at
once — that would take ten minutes and you would never open the
app again.

Instead you get keys. Three on the day you join, then one more
every day at midnight UTC. You choose which number to spend each
key on. Every number shows you a hint before you commit, so the
choice is yours and it is a real one.

You can bank up to three keys, so missing a day costs you nothing.
Come back on Friday and you have a decision to make about how to
spend the week.

WHAT GATI COUNTS

Time — days alive, hours alive, weeks old, months old, seasons
lived, full moons you have seen, your exact age to the second.

Body — heartbeats, breaths taken, steps walked, litres of blood
pumped, hours slept, full days spent asleep.

Habits — cups of coffee, glasses of water, meals eaten, screen time,
full days lost to your phone, hours of music, hours commuting.

Every figure is built from answers you give during a two-minute
setup, using published population averages for the things nobody
tracks. It is arithmetic, done honestly, and the app tells you
which assumptions it used.

THEN GO SOMEWHERE

A number on a screen is a fact. Gati turns it into a reason to
leave the house, by finding good places near you.

Unlock "hours of coffee consumed" and Wander shows you a coffee
place near you that is actually good and actually close — ranked
on distance, rating, how many people rated it, and whether it is
open right now, not just whichever chain paid the most. Unlock
your step count and it finds somewhere worth walking to.

Rate the places you visit. Save the ones you want to return to.

YOUR STORY

Every number you open lands on a timeline, with the date you
found it and room to write what you were doing. Build a streak.
Hit milestones. Look back after a month and see a record of the
year rather than a list of statistics.

BUILT PROPERLY

• Works fully without an account — everything lives on your phone
• No ads, anywhere, ever
• No analytics SDK, no tracking, no advertising ID
• Your approximate location is used to find places and is never
  stored against you
• Sign in only if you want your data on a second device
• Delete everything from inside the app, in two taps

A NOTE ON THE NUMBERS

Gati is not a fitness tracker and not a medical app. It does not
measure your body — it does arithmetic on what you told it. The
numbers are estimates, and good ones, but they are estimates.
Nothing here is health advice.
