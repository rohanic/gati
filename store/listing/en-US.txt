FULL DESCRIPTION  (2892/4000)
──────────────────────────────────────────────
You have taken about 300 million breaths. Your heart has beaten over
2 billion times. You have slept through roughly a third of your life,
and you have spent whole months of it looking at a screen.

You know none of these numbers. Gati works them out.

ONE NUMBER A DAY

Gati holds 25 numbers about your life. You do not get them all at once —
that would take ten minutes and you would never open the app again.

Instead you get keys. Three on the day you join, then one more every day
at midnight. You choose which number to spend each key on, and every
number shows you a hint before you commit, so the choice is a real one.

You can bank up to three keys, so missing a day costs you nothing. Come
back on Friday and you have a decision to make about your week.

WHAT GATI COUNTS

Time — days alive, hours alive, weeks and months old, seasons lived,
full moons you have seen, your exact age to the second.

Body — heartbeats, breaths taken, steps walked, litres of blood pumped,
hours of sleep, full days spent asleep.

Habits — cups of coffee, glasses of water, meals eaten, screen time,
full days lost to your phone, hours of music, hours commuting.

Every figure is built from answers you give during a two-minute setup,
using published population averages for the things nobody tracks. It is
arithmetic, done honestly, and the app tells you which assumptions it used.

THEN GO SOMEWHERE NEAR YOU

A number on a screen is a fact. Gati turns it into a reason to leave the
house, by finding good places near you.

Unlock your coffee total and Wander shows you a coffee place nearby that
is actually good and actually close — ranked on distance, rating, how
many people rated it, and whether it is open right now. Unlock your step
count and it finds somewhere worth walking to.

You choose how far to look, from 2 km to 50 km. Rate the places you
visit. Save the ones you want to return to.

YOUR STORY

Every number you open lands on a timeline, with the date you found it
and room to write what you were doing. Build a streak. Hit milestones.
Look back after a month and see a record of your year rather than a list
of statistics.

BUILT PROPERLY

• Works fully without an account — everything lives on your phone
• No ads, anywhere
• No analytics, no tracking, no advertising ID
• Your location is used only for the place search you ask for, and never stored
  against you
• Sign in only if you want your data on a second device
• Delete everything from inside the app, in two taps

A NOTE ON THE NUMBERS

Gati is not a fitness tracker and not a medical app. It does not measure
your body — it does arithmetic on what you told it. The numbers are
estimates, and good ones, but they are estimates. Nothing here is health
advice.

Gati means motion, pace, the way something moves forward.
